    How to read the diagram

    This calculator illustrates Bayesian updating: evidence updates a prior opinion into a posterior range. Add items to compare the combined weight of evidence with that range.

    Decibans
    A logarithmic unit for expressing the weight of evidence. Positive values support a claim, negative values count against it, and +10 dBan multiplies the odds by ten.
    Priors and posteriors
    Set a prior range before adding evidence. The diagram shows the posterior range after the combined weight is applied.
    Dissonance
    The proportion of absolute evidence weight canceled by opposing weights. It reaches 100% when the weights cancel.
